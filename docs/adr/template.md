# ADR-NNNN — Title

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR-XXXX
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** Andy (product) + Vibe (engineering)
- **Context:** what situation forces a decision?
- **Decision:** what we decided, in one paragraph.
- **Consequences:** what becomes easier, what becomes harder.
- **Rules/invariants introduced:** RULE-XXX-NNN / INV-XXX-NNN ids (registered in docs/04-conventions.md).
- **Alternatives considered:** multirepo vs monorepo, etc., and why rejected.

ADRs are immutable once accepted. To change a decision, write a new ADR that supersedes.
