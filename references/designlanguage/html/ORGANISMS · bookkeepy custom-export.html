Account card
bookkeepy custom organism
component v1.3.0
what it is
Your draft’s account card, rebuilt: name and period on top, the balance as the hero, and diverging bars below (money in up in green, out down in red, grey where there was no activity). The period chip switches the bar grain between weeks and days.
variants
interaction
rest · hover (white-a12 hairline) · selected (accent hairline) · focus ring
privacy
hidden: balance becomes ••••, bars keep their shape
period
last 3 mo / w · last 3 mo / d (chip)
loading
skeleton bars + balance (first load)

bookkeepy custom organism
component v1.3.0
what it is
The overview’s list of things waiting on you. Each row is a link into the board: clicking flies the camera to that place. Tone says why it’s here (accent = needs a decision, warning = near a limit, neutral = reminder).
variants
row
icon (regular → duotone on hover) · title · source caption · chevron
tone
accent · warning · neutral (icon color only)
interaction
rest · hover · pressed · focus
done
checked rows fade out after duration-slow
empty
all caught up (Empty, success glyph)

Board place
bookkeepy custom organism
component v1.3.0
what it is
A destination on the canvas: a card that frames one place (overview, transactions, accounts…). The camera lands on it; the sidebar highlights it. A header names it and holds its actions; the body hosts the place’s own organisms.
variants
place
rest · hover (white-a12) · active (accent hairline + “you are here”) · dimmed (25%, focus elsewhere)
header
region › name · actions (open full, more)
body
slot: any organism (account cards, table, to-do)

D5
Minimap
bookkeepy custom organism
component v1.3.0
what it is
The board in miniature, bottom-right in the HUD: regions as tiles, places as blocks, notes as dots, and an accent frame for what the camera sees. Drag the frame to pan; click a tile to fly there.
variants
rest
136 × 72, hud fill, regions neutral-800, places neutral-650
flying
viewport frame moves with the camera; destination place in accent-subtle
hover
the hovered tile brightens (white-a12)
masters · pen.dev components
rest · flying to transactions · hover a tile
D6
Timeline scrubber
bookkeepy custom organism
component v1.3.0
what it is
Time travel for the whole board. Months on a rail: the past is a solid line, the future dotted, now an orange mark. Drag the thumb to rewind or look ahead; places repaint and the floor changes (warm past, cool forecast).
variants
day
today (white thumb on now) · past (sand thumb, bubble) · future (steel thumb, bubble)
bubble
month and year above the thumb while dragging
keys
← → a month · Home = now

D7
Day rail
bookkeepy custom organism
component v1.3.0
what it is
The time layer’s calendar, printed along the top edge of the board: one day-tick per day, events pinned above their day, the month name at the start. It repaints when you scrub to another month.
variants
ticks
day-tick × 28–31 with theme { day } per tick
events
category / money dots above the tick (rent · payday · Netflix)
month
overline at the start; past month in time-past, forecast in time-future
