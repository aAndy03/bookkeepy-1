bookkeepy · organisms · chart / plot
Charts: money over time, money by kind.
Data colors data-1…8 for categories, money-in / money-out for direction, money-forecast for what hasn’t happened yet. Gridlines are dividers; labels are caption type in subtle foreground; numbers use tabular figures.
