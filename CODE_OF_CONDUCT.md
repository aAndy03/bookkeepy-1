# Code of Conduct — Bookkeepy 1

We use the [Contributor Covenant 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment: using welcoming and
inclusive language; being respectful of differing viewpoints; accepting constructive
criticism gracefully; focusing on what is best for the community and for users' financial
well-being.

Unacceptable behavior includes: harassment, trolling, derogatory comments, publishing
others' private information, and anything illegal.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately via GitHub (block/report) or a Security Advisory
for sensitive matters (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)). The project owner decides and
enforces. Responses are private, fair, and documented.
